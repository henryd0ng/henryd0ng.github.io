RoboDojo task media

These files include native RoboDojo scene captures, recorded simulation views, and static views rendered from saved RoboDojo layouts and USD assets.
Published WebP scene images are resized/encoded versions of those captures. Recording thumbnails use same-session native head-camera first frames; when the original per-camera recording is no longer available, the exact head-camera panel is cropped from the same first montage frame and encoded losslessly. Published MP4s preserve the recorded video stream; the head-camera hero is the full original camera view without cropping or time changes.

Some scene objects originate from NVIDIA, SimReady Assets (nvidia/simready-assets), revision 7879e0e50423290300ac20ecd6e727325f680b60, licensed CC BY 4.0.
